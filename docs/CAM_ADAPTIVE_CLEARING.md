# Circular adaptive clearing in Whittle

FreeCAD reference is 1.1.0 at commit `34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf`. Adaptive2d takes stock, target boundaries, and previously cleared paths. It uses Clipper 6.4.2 for planar geometry and estimates newly swept cutting area against the cleared area. The general solver adjusts the direction of successive moves toward a target cut area per distance and tracks cleared versus uncleared links. Its native solver is LGPL-2.1-or-later. Whittle uses original TypeScript with the disk-overlap model below. This increment adds no dependency. [Adaptive2d source](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/libarea/Adaptive.cpp) · [Adaptive2d interface](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/libarea/Adaptive.hpp)

In Manufacturing, use **Add adaptive clearing**, pick a sketch containing one circle, and assign a flat cutter. Enter depth, clearance, and optional maximum stepdown. Target radial engagement defaults to 15% of cutter diameter and accepts 1–50%. The operation persists in `.cam`.

Each depth starts with a center plunge by default. The [helical entry option](CAM_ADAPTIVE_HELIX.md) descends on a fitted orbit and feeds to the center. The planner treats the plunge as an initial cleared disk and each completed ring as an expansion of that disk. It finds the largest next ring whose exposed cutter area, computed by circular disk overlap, is at most the area of a circular segment at the requested radial engagement. Rings open outwards and the last ring meets the cutter-compensated wall. Straight feed links between rings and analytic circular cuts use Whittle's existing neutral motions. Per-pass plunge, retract, clearance travel, and TIP playback use the existing motion planner.

This page describes the analytic single-circle strategy. See [adaptive clearing for concave pockets and islands](CAM_ADAPTIVE_REGIONS.md) for the polygonal cleared-area planner. Both assume the pocket material is solid at each depth. The engagement target applies after entry. Entry requires a suitable cutter and stock condition. The [stock-aware linking option](CAM_ADAPTIVE_LINKS.md) considers completed cuts at the current depth. Pre-cleared stock, assembly collision checking, and NC export remain outside the adaptive workflow. General adaptive clearing would need an explicit swept-area model for arbitrary boundaries and a way to select and validate local engagement moves against it.
