# Whittle Phase 04B · Pocket milling

FreeCAD reference is 1.1.0 at commit `34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf`. PocketShape groups selected horizontal faces, derives outlines from connected vertical walls, and builds removal volumes between start and final depth. The shared Area operation slices or projects those volumes at pass heights. Native `Area::makePocket` dispatches offset, spiral, zigzag, and other strategies. Offset passes use the cutter radius and stepover, and native zigzag delegates to libarea's pocket generator. PocketShape and native `App/Area.cpp` are LGPL-2.1-or-later. The pinned libarea area code is BSD-3-Clause and its bundled Clipper 6.4.2 is BSL-1.0. Whittle uses original TypeScript and adds no upstream code or dependencies. [PocketShape.py](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/Path/Op/PocketShape.py) · [Area.py](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/Path/Op/Area.py) · [Native Area](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/App/Area.cpp) · [libarea](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD/blob/34a9716668b1ddeb55b914f1c5be644826bdbbbf/src/Mod/CAM/libarea/Area.cpp)

In Manufacturing, add **Pocket milling** to a setup context, pick a closed native sketch, and assign a tool position with an inherited flat cutter TYPE and CUTTER_DIAMETER. Depth and clearance are measured from the sketch plane. Enter maximum stepdown for multiple Z passes. Stepover defaults to 50% of cutter diameter and accepts values above zero through 50%. The operation persists in `.cam` and shares neutral line and arc motions, the viewport, and TIP-anchored tool assembly playback with contour milling.

A convex straight-sided boundary is inset by intersecting the inward half-planes at one cutter radius. Alternating rows and their feed connections stay inside that convex region, with a finishing perimeter at each depth. A circle clears from the center with concentric analytic arcs and short radial feeds. The common motion planner feeds vertically to each pass, retracts vertically, and makes inter-pass lateral rapid moves at clearance.

The initial version accepted one convex polygon or one circle. See [concave pockets and islands](CAM_POCKET_OFFSETS.md) for the newer offset strategy. Ramps, helices, stock-aware linking, and pocket NC export are outside the pocket workflow. Internal polygon corners retain the material a round cutter cannot reach. Entry is a vertical plunge and requires a suitable cutter and entry location. Clearance is user-entered, and the preview does not check stock, shank, holder, or fixture collisions.
